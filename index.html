When and where is camp?

Friday, January 29 through Sunday, January 31, 2027, at Camp Pondo in Running Springs, California. The camp address is 31324 Green Valley Lake Road, Running Springs, CA 92382. Citizens departure and return arrangements are still to be confirmed.

Who is this weekend for?

Pondo lists this as a combined junior high and high school weekend. Citizens will confirm eligible grades and group details before registration.

What will it cost?

Church pricing, what’s included, payment details, and any financial-assistance information will be shared when confirmed. This interest form does not collect payment.

Does this reserve a spot?

No. Expressing interest helps Citizens know who would like more information. It is not camp registration, a reservation, or a payment commitment.

What about transportation and packing?

Transportation, departure and return times, packing guidance, supervision details, and registration deadlines will be confirmed by Citizens. Pondo’s activities can change with the weather.
